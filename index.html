Learning Objectives

学习目标

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

学完本单元后，你将能够：
  • identify the purposes of various visuals for presenting IT statistical data
  • describe quantitative findings (distributions, pre- and post-project comparisons, changes and trends)

识别各种视觉图表在呈报IT统计数据中的用途 | 描述量化研究结果（分布情况、项目前后比较、变化和趋势） | 在评估报告中清晰客观地选取和描述显著发现

Quantitative findings are the numerical data collected using tools such as questionnaire surveys. They are expressed in numbers, percentages, or statistics, and provide measurable evidence to assess a project's effectiveness objectively. Visuals help people see comparisons, relationships, or trends clearly.

量化研究结果是使用问卷调查等工具收集的数值数据，以数字、百分比或统计数据表示，提供可量化的证据以客观评估项目成效。视觉图表有助于人们清晰地看到比较、关系或趋势。

Task 2a: Describing Distributions

任务二a：描述分布情况
Sample Formula: [General description] + [Segment description] + [Conjunction] + [Numerical description] + [Segment description]
Example: "More than half of the respondents checked their online systems for potential threats on a weekly basis, whereas 16% of them did so monthly."
描述饼图或百分比分布时，可将数字描述（如60%）与概括描述（如「超过半数受访者」）结合使用。

Reporting Grammatical Adjustments:

报告时的语法调整：
  • 'you' → 'respondents' / 'participants'
  • 'check' → 'checked' (simple past tense)
  • 'your' → 'their'
  • 'did so' to avoid repeating the full phrase

任务二b：描述项目前后的结果

Example 2: "About one-third of them did so every two weeks, compared to 10% prior to the project."
项目前后数据通过比较干预前后的态度或习惯来衡量影响。

Task 2c: Describing Changes and Trends

任务二c：描述变化和趋势
Trend Analysis Examples:
• "The percentage of clients who agreed that the prices of anti-virus software products were fair rose sharply from 23% two years ago to 77% this year."
• "The percentage of shoppers who thought the interface of our online store was poor fell remarkably from 97% to 17%."
• "The percentage of customers who wanted more product tutorials remained stable over the past three years (45% → 46% → 45%)."
• Contrast: "The number of respondents who ran anti-virus scans 'quite often' increased sharply from 5% to 90%, while those running scans 'occasionally' dropped significantly from 95% to 7%."
趋势分析范例：使用上升/下降动词（如rise、drop）搭配程度副词（如sharply、remarkably）来描述变化。用contrast连接词（如while、whereas）对比不同趋势。

While quantitative data provides measurable evidence of an IT project's outcomes, qualitative data reveals the reasons and user perspectives behind system adoption and performance. Reporting qualitative findings complements metrics with human insights.

量化数据提供项目成果的可量化证据，而质性数据（意见、开放式回答、访谈）则揭示数据背后的原因和洞察。你将学习报告质性研究结果，以人文视角补充数据驱动的结果。

Task 3: Reporting Negative Comments Tactfully

任务三：委婉报告负面评价
To provide a balanced evaluation, negative comments must be reported to pinpoint areas for improvement. Use neutral, professional, and constructive language rather than harsh or critical wording.
为提供平衡的评估，必须报告负面评论以指出改进领域。使用中立、专业和建设性的语言，而非严厉或批评性的措辞。

Comparison:

  • Harsh: "No support outside the workshops! I feel completely abandoned!"
  • Version A (Direct): "Some expressed that there was no support outside the workshops. They felt completely abandoned."
  • Version B (Tactful & Constructive): "Some thought the learning process would be more effective with additional support outside the workshops."

Techniques:

TechniqueDirect / Rude FeedbackTactful Reporting
1. Replace rude/offensive words with neutral terms'The course fees are a complete RIP-OFF!'Some students thought that the digital audio production course fees were somewhat high.
2. Focus on the issue, not the person'The instructor spoke so fast; I couldn't follow the steps.'Feedback indicated that the instruction pace was quite fast, making certain technical steps difficult to follow.
3. Use frequency words to show non-constant issues'If I can't book a digital audio workstation through the online system, how am I supposed to practise?'It was reported that users occasionally experienced difficulties booking digital audio workstations through the online system.
4. Highlight constructive elements'The front desk was a disaster! The staff were so rude when I asked about schedules!'Feedback revealed that customer service communication at the front desk could be improved.

Evaluation Report on ‘Data Analytics Basics for Nonprofits’ Project P. 9–11 (1/3)

「Data Analytics Basics for Nonprofits」项目评估报告

1. Introduction

1. 简介

As the importance of data analytics continues to grow in today’s data-centric world, many local non-profit organisations in Hong Kong find themselves lacking the necessary skills to harness data for organisational growth. This mini-project, ‘Data Analytics Basics for Nonprofits’, sets out to improve their understanding of data analytics and provide them with practical skills by introducing basic analytical tools and techniques. This report evaluates the project’s effectiveness and offers recommendations for future enhancements.

随着数据分析在当今以数据为中心的世界日益重要，香港许多本地非营利机构发现自己缺乏善用数据以促进机构发展所需的技能。迷你项目「Data Analytics Basics for Nonprofits」旨在透过介绍基本分析工具和技巧，提升他们对数据分析的认识，并让他们掌握实用技能。本报告评估项目成效，并就未来改进提出建议。

2.1 Participants

Evaluation Report on ‘Data Analytics Basics for Nonprofits’ Project P. 12–13 (2/3)

「Data Analytics Basics for Nonprofits」项目评估报告

3. Findings

3. 研究结果

Slightly more than two-thirds of the participants reported that they were familiar with data analytics tools after the project, which was a notable rise from 5% prior to it. Approximately a quarter of them believed that they were very familiar with them, compared to just 12% before the project began. Additionally, the number of participants who felt fairly confident in creating basic data visualisations rose drastically from 15% to 80%, whereas the number of participants who were not confident fell remarkably from 80% to 5% after the project.

项目结束后，略多于三分之二的参与者表示熟悉数据分析工具，较项目前的5%显著上升。约四分之一的参与者认为自己非常熟悉，而项目开始前只有12%。此外，感到相当有信心制作基本数据可视化图表的参与者由15%大幅上升至80%，而没有信心的参与者则由80%显著下降至5%。

3. Findings (cont’d)

3. 研究结果（续）

Participants (also) praised the real-world dataset exercises as highly practical and easy to understand. However, a few participants faced issues with outdated devices during the hands-on exercises, which hindered their learning experience. In addition, some other participants thought that it would be helpful if more data and examples specific to non-profit organisations could be used.

参与者亦称赞真实数据集练习非常实用，也容易理解。然而，少数参与者在实践练习中遇到设备过时的问题，妨碍了学习体验。此外，其他一些参与者认为，如能使用更多针对非营利机构的数据和例子，会更有帮助。

4. Discussion

4. 讨论

The findings demonstrate that the five workshops have effectively boosted participants’ data analytics knowledge and cultivated a mindset of analysing data. With improved skills in using analytical tools, participants now regularly analyse data, interpret visualisations, spot trends, and make informed decisions, all of which have enhanced their operational efficiency. In sum, the project has evidently fostered a data-driven mindset among these participants.

研究结果显示，五节工作坊有效提升了参与者的数据分析知识，并培养了分析数据的思维。随着使用分析工具的技能提升，参与者现在会定期分析数据、解读可视化图表、发现趋势并作出明智决定，这一切都提高了他们的运作效率。总括而言，项目明显培养了这些参与者的数据驱动思维。

5. Conclusion

5. 结论

The findings demonstrate that the project has been highly effective in boosting participants’ data analytics capabilities and empowering them to make data-driven decisions. While similar workshops should continue to be offered, there are opportunities to refine the project for greater impact.

研究结果显示，项目在提升参与者的数据分析能力、让他们作出数据驱动决策方面非常有效。虽然应继续举办类似工作坊，但项目仍有改进空间，以发挥更大影响。

6. Recommendations

6. 建议

To build on this success, it is recommended that regular workshops be organised to reach more local non-profit organisations, allowing a broader audience to benefit, while experienced participants can mentor newcomers and share practical insights.

为延续这一成功，建议定期举办工作坊，让更多本地非营利机构受惠，同时由经验丰富的参与者指导新加入者并分享实用心得。

Additionally, it would be ideal to send participants a checklist covering software requirements, troubleshooting tips, etc., prior to the workshop to minimise technical disruptions. To increase the practicality of the project for participants, case studies specific to the context of non-profit organisations could be used to enhance relevance to their actual operations.

此外，最好在工作坊举行前向参与者发送清单，列明软件要求、疑难排解提示等，以减少技术干扰。为提高项目对参与者的实用性，可使用针对非营利机构情境的个案研究，以增强内容与其实际运作的相关性。
