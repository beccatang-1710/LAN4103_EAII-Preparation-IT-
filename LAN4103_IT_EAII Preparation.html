Quantitative findings are the numerical data collected using tools such as questionnaire surveys. They are expressed in numbers, percentages, or statistics, and provide measurable evidence to assess a project's effectiveness objectively. Visuals help people see comparisons, relationships, or trends clearly.

量化研究结果是使用问卷调查等工具收集的数值数据，以数字、百分比或统计数据表示，提供可量化的证据以客观评估项目成效。视觉图表有助于人们清晰地看到比较、关系或趋势。

Task 2a: Describing Distributions

任务二a：描述分布情况
Sample Formula: [General description] + [Segment description] + [Conjunction] + [Numerical description] + [Segment description]
Example: "More than half of the respondents checked their online systems for potential threats on a weekly basis, whereas 16% of them did so monthly."
描述饼图或百分比分布时，可将数字描述（如60%）与概括描述（如「超过半数受访者」）结合使用。

Reporting Grammatical Adjustments:

报告时的语法调整：
  • 'you' → 'respondents' / 'participants'
  • 'check' → 'checked' (simple past tense)
  • 'your' → 'their'
  • 'did so' to avoid repeating the full phrase

Task 2b: Describing Pre- and Post-Project Findings

任务二b：描述项目前后的结果
Example 1: "More than half of the respondents checked their online systems for potential threats on a weekly basis, which was a significant improvement from only 2% before the project."
Example 2: "About one-third of them did so every two weeks, compared to 10% prior to the project."
项目前后数据通过比较干预前后的态度或习惯来衡量影响。

Practice 1:

Once a month: Pre 0% → Post 5% | Once in three months: Pre 5% → Post 60% | Once in six months: Pre 10% → Post 25% | Never: Pre 85% → Post 10%

Disagree: Pre 96% → Post 0% | Somewhat agree: Pre 4% → Post 60% | Strongly agree: Pre 0% → Post 40%

Practice 3:

"Are you satisfied with the performance of the new AI agent?"

Very satisfied: Pre 7% → Post 75% | Satisfied: Pre 30% → Post 20% | Not satisfied: Pre 63% → Post 5%

Task 2c: Describing Changes and Trends

任务二c：描述变化和趋势
Trend Analysis Examples:
• "The percentage of clients who agreed that the prices of anti-virus software products were fair rose sharply from 23% two years ago to 77% this year."
• "The percentage of shoppers who thought the interface of our online store was poor fell remarkably from 97% to 17%."
• "The percentage of customers who wanted more product tutorials remained stable over the past three years (45% → 46% → 45%)."
• Contrast: "The number of respondents who ran anti-virus scans 'quite often' increased sharply from 5% to 90%, while those running scans 'occasionally' dropped significantly from 95% to 7%."
趋势分析范例：使用上升/下降动词（如rise、drop）搭配程度副词（如sharply、remarkably）来描述变化。用contrast连接词（如while、whereas）对比不同趋势。

Vocabulary: qualitative (adjective): relating to what something is like or how good it is, rather than how much of it there is

While quantitative data provides measurable evidence of an IT project's outcomes, qualitative data reveals the reasons and user perspectives behind system adoption and performance. Reporting qualitative findings complements metrics with human insights.

量化数据提供项目成果的可量化证据，而质性数据（意见、开放式回答、访谈）则揭示数据背后的原因和洞察。你将学习报告质性研究结果，以人文视角补充数据驱动的结果。

Task 3: Reporting Negative Comments Tactfully

任务三：委婉报告负面评价
To provide a balanced evaluation, negative comments must be reported to pinpoint areas for improvement. Use neutral, professional, and constructive language rather than harsh or critical wording.
为提供平衡的评估，必须报告负面评论以指出改进领域。使用中立、专业和建设性的语言，而非严厉或批评性的措辞。

Comparison:

  • Harsh: "No support outside the workshops! I feel completely abandoned!"
  • Version A (Direct): "Some expressed that there was no support outside the workshops. They felt completely abandoned."
  • Version B (Tactful & Constructive): "Some thought the learning process would be more effective with additional support outside the workshops."

Techniques:

TechniqueDirect / Rude FeedbackTactful Reporting
1. Replace rude/offensive words with neutral terms'The course fees are a complete RIP-OFF!'Some students thought that the digital audio production course fees were somewhat high.
2. Focus on the issue, not the person'The instructor spoke so fast; I couldn't follow the steps.'Feedback indicated that the instruction pace was quite fast, making certain technical steps difficult to follow.
3. Use frequency words to show non-constant issues'If I can't book a digital audio workstation through the online system, how am I supposed to practise?'It was reported that users occasionally experienced difficulties booking digital audio workstations through the online system.
4. Highlight constructive elements'The front desk was a disaster! The staff were so rude when I asked about schedules!'Feedback revealed that customer service communication at the front desk could be improved.

1. Introduction

The booming mobile gaming industry has sparked teenagers' interest in game development. Despite this growing enthusiasm, many lack the technical knowledge and creative skills needed to design functional and engaging games. The objective of the 'Mobile Game Development Basics' project is to teach teenagers foundational skills in game design, programming, and problem-solving through hands-on workshops. This report assesses the effectiveness of the project and provides suggestions for future improvement.

蓬勃发展的手机游戏行业激发了青少年对游戏开发的兴趣。尽管热情高涨，但许多人缺乏设计功能性和吸引力游戏所需的技术知识和创意技能。「手机游戏开发基础」项目的目标是通过实践工作坊教授青少年游戏设计、编程和问题解决的基础技能。本报告评估项目成效并为未来改进提供建议。

2. Methodology

2.1 Participants

Three professional game developers were recruited as trainers, while 40 teenagers from various secondary schools in Hong Kong were invited to participate in the project.

三名专业游戏开发者获聘为培训师，40名来自香港各中学的青少年受邀参加项目。

2.2 Implementation

From January to February 202-, five workshops were conducted by the trainers. These sessions covered topics such as coding basics using the Unity game engine, designing user interfaces, implementing game mechanics, and testing functionality. Participants engaged in practical exercises, including prototyping simple games and troubleshooting common issues.

一月至二月期间，培训师举办了五节工作坊。课程涵盖使用Unity游戏引擎的编程基础、用户界面设计、游戏机制实现和功能测试等主题。参与者进行了实践练习，包括制作简单游戏原型和排除常见问题。

2.3 Evaluation

To evaluate the project, participants had been invited to complete a pre-project survey in December 202- assessing their prior knowledge of game development. In March 202-, a post-project survey was administered, and online group discussions were held to gather feedback on their experiences and challenges faced during the workshops.

Evaluation Report on 'Cantonese for All' Project

1. Introduction

The non-Chinese speaking (NCS) students who are new to Hong Kong often face challenges in integrating due to language barriers. The mini-project, 'Cantonese for All', aims to help these students learn Cantonese to improve their daily interactions and community integration. This report discusses the effectiveness of the project and provides recommendations for future initiatives.

新来的非华语学生因语言障碍往往面临融入社区的挑战。「人人学广东话」迷你项目旨在帮助这些学生学习广东话，改善日常交流和社区融合。本报告讨论项目成效并为未来活动提供建议。

2. Methodology

2.1 Participants

Two instructors were recruited to deliver the course, while 50 new NCS students from Hong Kong Higher Institute were invited to participate in the course.

两名导师获聘授课，50名来自香港专上学院的新非华语学生受邀参加课程。

2.2 Implementation

From October to November 202-, twelve 1-hour lessons were conducted by the instructors. These lessons focused on teaching the NCS students useful daily Cantonese expressions, such as greetings, ordering food, and asking for directions, in addition to some highlights of local culture.

十月至十一月期间，导师共举办了十二节一小时的课程。课程重点教授非华语学生实用的日常广东话表达，如问候、点餐和问路，同时介绍本地文化亮点。

2.3 Evaluation

To collect data for evaluation, the NCS students completed a pre-project survey in September 202-. In December, they took part in a post-project survey and focus group interviews to share their experiences.

为收集评估数据，非华语学生于九月完成了项目前调查。十二月，他们参加了项目后调查和焦点小组访谈，分享他们的体验。
